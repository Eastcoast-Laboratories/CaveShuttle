### Full description (4,000 characters max)
Pilot a spaceship through dangerous caves, rescue the pod, and escape into the sky.

Cave Shuttle is a physics-based arcade game about precision flying, fuel management, and momentum control. Navigate through cave levels, dodge walls and enemy fire, dock the pod, and deliver it back upward into open sky to complete the level.

FEATURES

- Physics-based flight with gravity, inertia, and momentum
- Several cave levels with distinct layouts and hazards
- Enemy bunkers, bullets, fuel depots and slider doors
- Single-player mode
- Two-player mode: one player controls the ship, the second player contoros the gun and later in the game the pod with its own gun and thruster
- Bonus lifes
- Sound effects
- Named high scores
- Time bonuses for fast level completion
- Custom level-pack support and an integrated level editor with export function
- Local import and export of player data

HOW TO PLAY

Use the on-screen touch buttons for acceleration, rotation, the tractor beam, and firing. Touch controls can be toggled and their opacity adjusted in the settings menu.

1. Fly close to the pod.
2. Activate the tractor beam to dock it under your ship.
3. Fly upward into the sky with the pod to finish the level.
4. Earn more points by flying efficiently, collecting fuel, triggering level elements, destroying bunkers, and finishing quickly.

HIGH SCORES

Pick a player name or use the generated one. Every level completion and full run is recorded with a detailed score breakdown. High-score tables are separated by level, level pack, and 1- or 2-player mode, so you always know exactly how your points were earned.

## Privacy policy text

Cave Shuttle may offer optional online accounts for public leaderboards and score synchronization. If a player creates an account, the chosen player name and associated high-score data are transmitted to and stored on the game server. Players can delete their account and associated data at any time through the in-game settings or by contacting the developer.

The game does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs. If a player loads a level pack from an external server, that server may process standard technical connection data such as an IP address, request time, and requested resource according to its own privacy policy.

For the Play Console privacy-policy field, use a publicly accessible HTTPS URL containing the complete privacy policy. The in-app privacy page is available at the `#/datenschutz` route, but the public URL must be verified after the website is deployed.

## Data safety questionnaire

- **Does the app collect personal user data?** Yes: player name and high-score data if the player creates an online account.
- **Does the app share personal user data with third parties?** No.
- **Does the app provide account creation or login?** Yes, optional online accounts for leaderboards and score synchronization.
- **Does the app contain ads?** No.
- **Does the app contain in-app purchases?** No.
- **Does the app store game data?** Yes: username, progress, settings, named high scores, score breakdowns, and installed level-pack data.
- **Can users delete game data?** Yes, through the in-game reset, by clearing app storage, by uninstalling the app, or by deleting the online account through the online settings.
